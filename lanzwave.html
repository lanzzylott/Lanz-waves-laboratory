Frequency

Frequency tells us how many complete wave cycles happen every second. It is measured in Hertz (Hz).

f = cycles / second

Amplitude

Amplitude describes the maximum displacement of a wave from its resting position.

Larger amplitude = greater displacement

Wavelength

Wavelength is the distance between matching points on consecutive waves.

v = fλ
